Contractors, freelancers, and digital professionals are just a few of the groups that make up a large segment of truly small businesses. The TrulySmall branding focuses mainly on solopreneurs and truly small businesses of 1–3 employees. For our initial release, we decided to focus on the feature that was most needed: sending out an invoice.

As the UX Lead of the TrulySmall Invoicing App, my goal was to finalize the branding of TrulySmall and scope out the MVP of the Invoicing app. I collaborated closely with the development team to launch the project in Jan 2021.

overview

TrulySmall Invoicesis a lightweight invoicing application designed for freelancers, contractors, and small business owners to handle billing without the complexity of traditional accounting systems. Available on web and mobile devices, it enables users to quickly create, customize, and send professional invoices and estimates, track payment statuses, collect digital payments, and set up automated payment reminders to maintain smooth cash flow on the go.

the backstory

problem

In a fiercely competitive industry we needed to be nimble and think hard about each feature we wanted to add, so we can launch quickly for Tax season and expand from there. This is how we started off with the Invoicing App.

process

Branding

For the TrulySmall product, I work alongside with an external brand designer and marketing coordinator to create the vision and meaning what we wanted the audience to view TrulySmall as.

Colour

For the colours of TrulySmall, the team and I discussed how we wanted our branding to be conveyed in our colours with growth of a business, starting off as a bud and blooming. Hence why our main colours are a dark brown instead of black and green to represent nature and a bud.

Characters

In order to make the branding feel more friendly, modern, yet approachable, we purchased a set of character and made some tweaks with the colours to fit with our branding. These characters are added to the website as well as within the Invoicing App.

Fonts

For the fonts, we were inspired by the character Wealthsimple and Hello Bonsai’s UI and branding was like. We like decided on rakkas to be the titles/headers to add some quirkiness to our site while Poppins is a very clean and cute like tone.
