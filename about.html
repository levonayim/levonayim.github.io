Hi! My name is Levona. Nice to meet you :)

I’m a Product Designer based in Vancouver, Canada. I have 7+ years of design experience in B2B enterprise software and user-centric B2C solutions.

Previously I was a Product Designer at FICO for 4.5 years focusing on the data side of the FICO Platform. I’ve also worked at Kashoo, Rectxt (now known as Keeyora), Mothers Matter Centre, City of Surrey, and Scentuals Body Care.

My journey started in chemistry, where I originally planned to pursue a career in food testing and laboratory research. After about a year in, I did some self-reflecting and research and realized chemistry is not something I’m passionate about and it was definitely not something I like to do for the rest of my life.

I did some exploring at my university, Simon Fraser University and found a graphic design course as an elective and ended up switching faculties. I embraced my passion for visual and product design and never looked back since.

Recently, I’ve been tinkering around and exploring the possibilities of artificial intelligence and how to utilize its positives into our workflow and expand new ideas.

I love to explore and experiment by being more hands-on and creating. I believe design is a way of thinking, as well as tool to communicate an idea to a broader audience. I’m passionate about finding solutions to real world problems, from complex social issues to improved experiences and services encountered in daily life.

On the side, I love drawing cartoon stickers/gifs (which you may have seen when you entered into this page or another page), and experimenting through vibe coding small apps. When I’m not designing, I enjoy lifting weights, hiking, and going on long walks.

I’m open to new opportunities!

If you’re hiring, looking to collaborate, or just want to connect, send me a message via LinkedIn or drop an email. :)
