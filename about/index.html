I’m a Product Designer based in Vancouver, Canada. I have 7+ years of design experience in B2B enterprise software and user-centric B2C solutions.

I love to explore and experiment by being more hands-on and creating. I believe design is a way of thinking, as well as tool to communicate an idea to a broader audience. I’m passionate about finding solutions to real world problems, from complex social issues to improved experiences and services encountered in daily life.

Recently, I’ve been tinkering around and exploring the possibilities of artificial intelligence and how to utilize its positives into our workflow and expand new ideas.

On the side, I love drawing cartoon stickers/gifs (which you may have seen when you entered into this page or another page), and experimenting through vibe coding small apps. When I’m not designing, I enjoy lifting weights, hiking, and going on long walks.
