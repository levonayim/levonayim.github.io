Terminal Style Portfolio

claude gemini html/css/js

Description

  • In collaboration with Gemini and occasionally Claude, I wanted to build a portfolio site that wasn’t the typical standard design.

Why I created this

  • In a time where product design is very saturated, I wanted to create something out of the ordinary, especially when most designers use the same portfolio UI template.

Issues I encountered

  • I sent this to a friend to take a look, and their first thought was that I’d sent them a virus.
  • Although the UI was adjusted to be viewable from the phone, it wasn’t the best experience due to the constrained UI sizing.
  • Keeping the terminal UI consistent throughout the site was hard, especially once a portfolio piece got content-heavy.

What I learned

  • Even though the industry boasts about Claude Code, if your project isn’t too big and your budget is tight, Gemini is still a good friend to use. Where there were steps in the code I didn’t understand, Gemini would break it down for me or send me the entire replacement code. It probably can’t build as complex experiences, but it’s a good option to experiment with.
